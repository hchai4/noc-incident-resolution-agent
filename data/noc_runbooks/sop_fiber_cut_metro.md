SOP-NET-01: Metro Optical Fiber Cut Incident Protocol
1. Scope & Symptoms
This standard operating procedure applies to complete Loss of Signal (LOS) or Optical Time-Domain Reflectometer (OTDR) reflection anomalies detected on metro terrestrial optical routes.

2. Standard Service Level Agreement (SLA)
Standard Recovery Objective: 4 Hours from physical alarm confirmation.

Primary Cause: Third-party construction excavation, municipal utility strikes, or conduit shearing.

3. Immediate Technical Actions
Confirm Loss of Signal (LOS) on dense wavelength division multiplexing (DWDM) transponder ports.

Dispatch emergency fiber-splicing field teams to the identified kilometer marker via OTDR triangulation.

Attempt dynamic optical rerouting via secondary diverse duct path if protection switching is enabled.

4. Mandatory Client Notification Cadence
Initial Alert: Within 15 minutes of confirmed physical line cut.

Progress Status Updates: Every 60 minutes until fusion splicing and testing are complete.

Tone Requirement: Technical, transparent, specifying localized geographical segment and field dispatch status.
