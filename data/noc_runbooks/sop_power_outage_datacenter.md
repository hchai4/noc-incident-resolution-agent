SOP-NET-03: Datacenter Primary Power Failure & Facility Transfer
1. Scope & Symptoms
Applies to commercial utility grid power collapse, automated transfer switch (ATS) failover, or UPS battery bank engagement at critical Point of Presence (PoP) or Core Telemetry Datacenters.

2. Standard Service Level Agreement (SLA)
Critical SLA Objective: Zero traffic disruption during Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) transition; 2 Hours maximum utility line isolation.

Primary Cause: Municipal power grid failure, lightning strikes, or localized transformer explosions.

3. Immediate Technical Actions
Verify automated diesel generator start and load transfer via building management system (BMS).

Monitor battery discharge duration and temperature on critical core switch chassis.

Notify regional utility provider emergency dispatch and secure on-site diesel fuel delivery if municipal outage exceeds 2 hours.

4. Mandatory Client Notification Cadence
Initial Alert: Within 20 minutes if secondary power systems are engaged.

Progress Status Updates: Every 45 minutes until commercial power grid restoration.

Tone Requirement: Reassuring, authoritative, emphasizing redundant infrastructure and generator reserve status.
