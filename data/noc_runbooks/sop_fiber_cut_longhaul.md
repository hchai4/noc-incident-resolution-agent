SOP-NET-06: Long-Haul Intercity Fiber Cut Incident Protocol
1. Scope & Symptoms
This standard operating procedure applies to complete Loss of Signal (LOS) or OTDR reflection events on long-haul / intercity DWDM spans (typically 80 km or more between regeneration sites), not metro loops inside a single metro area.

2. Standard Service Level Agreement (SLA)
Standard Recovery Objective: 8 Hours from physical alarm confirmation.

Primary Cause: Right-of-way excavation, railroad corridor work, or aerial span damage between cities.

3. Immediate Technical Actions
Confirm LOS on the long-haul line system, not on a metro access ring.

Dispatch a long-haul splice team to the OTDR kilometer marker; expect travel measured in hours, not city blocks.

Reroute via an alternate intercity diverse route or third-party IRU before attempting a local metro protection switch.

4. Mandatory Client Notification Cadence
Initial Alert: Within 20 minutes of confirmed long-haul span cut.

Progress Status Updates: Every 90 minutes until the intercity span is restored and BER is clean.

Tone Requirement: Technical, specifying the intercity pair (for example LAX–PHX) and that this is not a metro duct cut.
