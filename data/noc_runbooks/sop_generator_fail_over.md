SOP-NET-15: Diesel Generator Fail-to-Start Protocol
1. Scope & Symptoms
Applies when commercial power is already lost and the on-site diesel generator fails to start, fails to take load, or stops under load. UPS is discharging. This is not a cooling-only event and not a confirmed municipal restoration.

2. Standard Service Level Agreement (SLA)
Critical SLA Objective: Restore generator output before UPS autonomy expires (site-specific, often 15–30 minutes).

Primary Cause: Fuel system, starter battery, ATS not transferring, or generator mechanical trip.

3. Immediate Technical Actions
Confirm ATS position and whether the generator cranked. Do not wait on the utility.

Attempt one restart from the generator panel. If it fails, shed non-critical load immediately to extend UPS time.

Call the emergency generator vendor and fuel truck. Begin a controlled PoP evacuation of traffic if UPS minutes are under 10.

4. Mandatory Client Notification Cadence
Initial Alert: Within 10 minutes of fail-to-start.

Progress Status Updates: Every 15 minutes until generator is loaded or traffic is drained.

Tone Requirement: Power-backup specific. Name UPS remaining minutes. Do not claim the grid is back.
