SOP-NET-07: Planned Optical Fiber Maintenance Window Protocol
1. Scope & Symptoms
This standard operating procedure applies to scheduled fiber work (splice, re-route, or plant upgrade) that was ticketed in advance. Customers were already notified of a maintenance window. It does not apply to unplanned Loss of Signal (LOS) or emergency OTDR events.

2. Standard Service Level Agreement (SLA)
Standard Window: Complete work inside the published maintenance window (typically 4 hours overnight).

Primary Cause: Planned plant upgrade, scheduled splice, or carrier-initiated fiber relocation.

3. Immediate Technical Actions
Do not dispatch an emergency splice team. Confirm the change ticket ID and that the window is still open.

Place the circuit in planned-maintenance state and follow the pre-approved hitless or brief-hit procedure.

If the window will overrun, stop new splice work and roll back to the last known-good fiber assignment.

4. Mandatory Client Notification Cadence
Initial Alert: Sent before the window (usually T-24h). Do not send an emergency "fiber cut" notice.

Progress Status Updates: At window start, at midpoint, and at close.

Tone Requirement: Scheduled, non-urgent, citing the change ticket. Do not describe this as a construction strike or metro cut.
