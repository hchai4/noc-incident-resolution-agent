SOP-NET-05: Customer SLA Classification & Communication Standards
1. Client Classification Tiers
Tier 1 (Mission-Critical / Financial Institutions):

Guaranteed Restoration SLA: 4 Hours for physical layer, 1 Hour for routing/peering.

Update Cadence: Every 30 minutes during active outages.

Direct Account Executive notification required.

Tier 2 (Enterprise & SaaS Providers):

Guaranteed Restoration SLA: 6 Hours for physical layer, 2 Hours for routing/peering.

Update Cadence: Every 60 minutes.

Tier 3 (Standard Commercial & General Wholesale):

Guaranteed Restoration SLA: 8 Hours.

Update Cadence: Every 90 minutes.

2. Contractual Non-Hallucination Mandate
Incident notification drafts MUST NOT promise specific restoration timestamps unless verified and confirmed by field technicians in the active ticket.

If uncertain, the notification must specify: "Estimated restoration will be provided within the next scheduled update window."
